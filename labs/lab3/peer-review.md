# Peer-review guidelines for Lab 3

## Overview

For Lab 3, each group must participate in one **obligatory peer review session** during the final week of the course.

A **session** is 2 scheduled hours (45 + 45 minutes).
In each session, groups will be put together in **clusters** of up to 6 groups.
Each group in the cluster will have a **slot** of 10–15 minutes to present to the other groups in the cluster.

In each slot, the **presenting group** should:

- demonstrate their application in use
- show what additional features were implemented, and describe how they were implemented
- describe and reflect on their development process, in particular AI usage (or lack thereof)

During this time, the **non-presenting groups** are expected to ask questions and comment on the presenting group's solution.
Examples of things you can ask/comment about:

1. additional features from a user interface perspective
1. technical details about application implementation
1. code quality, modularity and maintainability
1. tools/services used and their cost in time/money
1. what worked well? what would you recommend?
1. what didn't work as expected? what should be avoided?

The slot should _not_ be a formal presentation with slides and questions only at the end.
Rather, the entire slot should be an interactive discussion between all members of the cluster.

When one group in the cluster has finished their slot, the next group in the cluster can present.

## Examination

- **All group members must be physically present** for the entire session that they registered for.
- If you have a valid reason for not attending, you must contact your examiner with an explanation as soon as possible. You will be given an alternative task instead.
- Teachers/TAs will be present to take attendance and also ask questions to the presenting groups.
- Even if your application is not complete (or not working), you must still attend the peer review session and present the work that you have done.
